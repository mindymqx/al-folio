The pinwheel Nebuliser is specifically designed for children with respiratory diseases. It features a soft color scheme and rounded, adorable design that is more appealing to children compared to traditional nebulizers. When the compressor operates, it generates airflow - one part is used for nebulizing the medication, while another part drives the central pinwheel to rotate, capturing children's attention and making the treatment process more engaging. This helps alleviate negative emotions that children may experience during treatment.
